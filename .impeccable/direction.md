# Approved direction contract

Mode: Operate. Audience: materials researchers inspecting traceable catalogue evidence and scalar tensile-property estimates.

Authority: the user-approved plan explicitly pins a dark neutral laboratory, restrained colour, prominent units, a central tensile specimen, left-hand catalogue and right-hand predictions. Build path: code-first, explicitly chosen by the user. No image comp or stock imagery is required for this scientific app.

Visual system: self-hosted Manrope for readable controls and prose; IBM Plex Mono only for IDs and measurements. Charcoal-green workspace with fine rules; sage for actions and support, muted gold for predictions, pale sage diamonds for measured points. No decorative marketing hero or card grid. The reusable 3D fixture is authored geometry, shown in a subdued gridded laboratory scene.

Primary task: select a documented record, inspect three predictions and their intervals, understand training/test membership and support, save up to four comparisons. Secondary surfaces: compare, local CSV measurements, data/model evidence. Provenance, limits and units must remain legible. No ballistic controls or inverse material design.

Quality bar: a precise laboratory instrument interface. Meaningful loading/error/unsupported states; no fabricated measurement curves. Mobile must preserve all controls with no horizontal page overflow. WebGL failure must preserve scalar results. Scene motion must stay explicitly illustrative, keyboard-operable, and optional.

Verification: Chromium and WebKit actual ONNX inference; 12 browser tests, 11 unit tests and four Python tests. Detector reported no mechanical findings. Final screenshots are under .impeccable/review. Scientific model performance and source licensing are documented independently in the model card and source manifest.

## STORY
